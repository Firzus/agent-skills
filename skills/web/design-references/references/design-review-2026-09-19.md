## Wave 3 completed and scope correction

The completed export matched all 59 active cards: 57 Like and 2 Dislike votes. Of the 16 wave-3 proposals, 14 were approved; Capacities and Akiflow were rejected. No notes were supplied. Rejected cards are retained in withdrawn history; 57 approved cards remain active.

The user found the selection too landing-page-heavy. Eleven of the sixteen proposals were marketing-hosted interface showcases, versus five directly accessible dashboards or editor surfaces. Subsequent waves must target directly accessible application views and usable public demos; exclude landing pages and marketing-hosted screenshots or video posters as substitutes. Preserve previous approvals rather than retroactively rejecting them. If access or quality limits reduce the next wave, report fewer references instead of filling it with showcases.
