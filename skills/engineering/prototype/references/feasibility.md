# Establish technical feasibility

State the measurable claim and the decision it supports. Identify representative runtime, inputs, device or service constraints, and an observable acceptance threshold. Use an existing requirement or ask for the material missing threshold; do not invent a success target after measuring.

Establish a baseline and isolate the variable being evaluated. Keep input and measurement conditions comparable. Repeat measurements when variability could change the conclusion and report the observed spread rather than a favorable sample alone.

Use the real relevant boundary for rendering, physics, interaction, integration, or performance. A mocked service or simplified model can answer only the questions it preserves. Keep credentials and data protected; external load and persistent effects require appropriate authorization.

Record commands or actions, configurations, input scale, observations, and limitations. Distinguish feasibility demonstrated in the experiment from reliability or capacity in an untested deployment.

**Done when:** the agreed claim is supported, contradicted, or explicitly unresolved by reproducible measurements, and no broader performance promise is inferred.
